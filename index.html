Mira Tartarotti

I am a DPhil student in Mathematics at the University of Oxford, supervised by Martin Bays and Ehud Hrushovski. Previously, I did the MSc in Mathematics and Foundations of Computer Science at Oxford, where I wrote a master's thesis supervised by Ehud Hrushovski.

My research interests are broadly around model theory. To name some, I have found much joy learning about continuous logic, distality, descriptive set theory and C*-algebras.

You can contact me at tartarotti(at)maths.ox.ac.uk.
